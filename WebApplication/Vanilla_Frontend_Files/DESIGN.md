## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an esteemed livestock and animal commerce ecosystem serving the Jordanian and regional market. It bridges grounded agricultural commerce with a contemporary, trustworthy digital trading platform.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Value
- **Grounded & Stately:** Rooted in the rich cultural tradition of Arabian horsemanship, pastoral heritage, and premium livestock trading.
- **Trustworthy & Verified:** Transparent, secure, and compliance-driven for high-value transactions (auctions in JOD, pedigree verification, veterinary certifications).
- **Warm & Welcoming:** Avoids the clinical detachment of generic SaaS while maintaining high efficiency, legible data hierarchy, and operational clarity.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
- **Tactile Modernism & Warm Earth-Tone Minimalism:** Combining tactile, warm off-white canvas backgrounds with crisp, sand-and-leather-toned borders, deep chestnut call-to-actions, and desert terracotta accents.
- **Visual Stature:** High contrast in typographic headlines paired with warm, low-fatigue secondary values. Clean, deep charcoal typography prevents glare under varied lighting conditions.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural Jordanian earth tones, rich leather saddlery, terracotta pottery, and desert sands, balanced with precise situational feedback states for fast-paced live auctions.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#643822`):** Rich Chestnut Brown used for dominant calls to action, active navigation indicators, and primary interactives.
- **Primary Hover/Deep (`#4D2817`):** Deeper burnished chestnut for pressed, focused, and high-emphasis hover states.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C25E2E`):** Terracotta clay used for urgent CTA alternates, warm price highlights, live attention hooks, and badge borders.
- **Secondary Bright (`#D96B27`):** Sunbaked ochre for countdown highlights and energetic auction triggers.
- **Dark Neutral (`#1F1B18`):** Deep charcoal brown, providing high-contrast readability without the sterile harshness of pure black.
- **Muted Neutral (`#78716C`):** Warm stone gray for secondary details, metadata, subheaders, and breadcrumb trails.

### Backgrounds & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#FDFBF7`):** Warm cream providing an inviting, natural surface.
- **Surface Alt / Container (`#FAF8F5`):** Soft sand-tinted container background used for card panels, spec grids, and inner inset wells.
- **Surface Dark / Footer (`#120F0D`):** Deep volcanic charcoal for anchors, footers, and inverted hero banners.
- **Border / Divider (`#E7E1D8`):** Gentle beige hairline borders that structure cards and tables without creating visual clutter.

### Status Badges & Indicators
- **Live Auction:** Background `#FEE2E2`, Border `#FCA5A5`, Text `#991B1B`, Pulse Dot `#DC2626`.
- **Ending Soon:** Background `#FEF3C7`, Border `#FCD34D`, Text `#92400E`, Icon Accent `#D97706`.
- **Starting Soon:** Background `#E0F2FE`, Border `#BAE6FD`, Text `#0369A1`.
- **Ended / Settled:** Background `#E7E5E4`, Border `#D6D3D1`, Text `#57534E`.
- **Health / Verified:** Background `#ECFDF5`, Border `#A7F3D0`, Text `#065F46`.

## Typography

The design system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across display, body, and transactional surfaces. Its generous x-height, contemporary geometric balance, and warm humanistic undertones deliver both friendly approachability and institutional authority.

### Typographic Rules
- **Numerical Currency Treatment:** Currency values (e.g., `4,500 JOD`) always render with `font-weight: 800` (`label-numeric-lg` or `headline-md`) using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so prices and bids do not jitter as digits tick upwards.
- **Letter Spacing:** Tighter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) is preserved exclusively for large hero titles and card headlines to prevent visual fragmentation. Microcaps and metadata tags receive positive tracking (`+0.04em`) to ensure instant legibility on small badge pills.
- **Contrast Ratios:** Primary headlines and bid numbers use `#1F1B18` against `#FDFBF7` (yielding a AAA rating of >13:1). Muted secondary strings use `#78716C` (yielding a comfortable AA rating of >4.8:1).

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a standard 8-point spatial rhythm scaled with a maximum container threshold of `1280px` for optimal viewing on desktop monitors while preventing overly elongated listings.

### Responsive Grid
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 24px (`space-lg`) gutters, centered container with minimum 32px side margins. Auction catalog arranges in a balanced 3-column layout.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 24px margins. Auction catalog collapses gracefully to a 2-column configuration.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters, 16px outer edge padding. Cards transition to full-width single column cards with sticky bid trays.

### Rhythm & Density
- **Micro Spacing (`space-2xs` to `space-xs`):** Inner badge padding, icon-to-label gaps, and bid increment metadata clusters.
- **Component Padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Auction card body interior, table row heights, form input field insets.
- **Structural Separation (`space-xl` to `space-3xl`):** Hero section vertical padding, catalog header-to-filter margins, and major overview section breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation follows a warm tactile philosophy that balances flat architectural structural borders with warm ambient illumination rather than synthetic cold drop-shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas / Ground):** Background `#FDFBF7`. No shadow. Demarcated by subtle `#E7E1D8` 1px border lines.
- **Level 1 (Listing Cards & Panels):** Surface `#FFFFFF` resting on `#FDFBF7`. Bordered by 1px `#E7E1D8`. Shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(77, 40, 23, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(77, 40, 23, 0.03)`. Gives the impression of natural parchment or pressed cardstock.
- **Level 2 (Hover State & Active Bidding Drawers):** Used when the user interacts with an auction listing. Shadow expands: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(77, 40, 23, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(77, 40, 23, 0.04)` with an optical slight translation of `-2px` on the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogues & Floating Bid Bar):** Heavy backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with warm shaded shadow: `0 20px 32px -6px rgba(31, 27, 24, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (`2`)** shape language, utilizing `0.5rem` (8px) for inputs and sub-elements, `1rem` (16px) for cards, and full pill contours (`9999px`) for badges, status chips, and high-priority action buttons.

### Component Radii Rules
- **Badges & Category Pills:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to communicate soft taggability and highlight liveliness.
- **Action Buttons:** Large primary and secondary CTA buttons utilize either continuous pill geometry (`rounded-full`) or smooth 12px (`rounded-xl`) depending on prominence. Header buttons (Sign In / Register) are pill-styled.
- **Cards & Media Wrappers:** Cards implement a rounded exterior contour of 16px (`rounded-2xl`). Media images adhere to the upper corner radii (`16px 16px 0 0`) seamlessly clipping imagery.
- **Input Fields & Form Selectors:** Structured at 10px-12px roundedness to guarantee ergonomic comfort when tapping on mobile touchscreens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Place Bid", "Register"):**
  - Background: `#643822` (Rich Chestnut Brown).
  - Hover: `#4D2817`.
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`, Semi-bold 15px.
  - Border: None.
  - Padding: `12px 24px`. Full pill (`9999px`) or `12px` radius.
- **Urgent / Outbid CTA ("Bid Now — Ending Soon"):**
  - Background: Terracotta Gradient (`#C25E2E` to `#D96B27`).
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`, Bold 15px.
- **Secondary / Ghost ("Sign In", "Notify Me"):**
  - Background: Transparent or `#FAF8F5`.
  - Border: 1.5px solid `#643822` or `#D6D3D1`.
  - Text: `#643822` or `#1F1B18`.
- **Disabled / Ended ("Auction Ended", "View Results"):**
  - Background: `#E7E5E4`. Text: `#78716C`. Cursor: Not-allowed.

### Status Badges & Auction Chips
- **Live Badge:**
  - Visuals: Soft pink pill (`#FEE2E2`), text `#991B1B`, border `#FCA5A5`.
  - Indicator: 6px circular dot `#DC2626` featuring an animated CSS ping/pulse effect.
- **Ending Soon Badge:**
  - Visuals: Warm amber pill (`#FEF3C7`), text `#92400E`, lightning bolt icon `#D97706`.
- **Starting Soon Badge:**
  - Visuals: Gentle sky pill (`#E0F2FE`), text `#0369A1`, clock icon.
- **Ended Badge:**
  - Visuals: Taupe gray pill (`#E7E5E4`), text `#57534E`.

### Auction Cards
- **Structure:**
  - Container: White background with 1px `#E7E1D8` border, 16px radius, overflow hidden.
  - Image Container: 16:10 aspect ratio, soft cream placeholder tone, image set to `object-fit: contain` or `cover`.
  - Status Overlay: Floating top-left badge absolute positioned at `12px 12px`.
  - Body Content: Title (`headline-sm`, 18px bold), breed/category line (`body-sm`, muted `#78716C`), location with pin icon (`Amman, Jordan`).
  - Pricing & Countdown Well: Separated with a subtle beige top-border (`#F3EFEA`), displaying Current Bid (`18px-24px` bold `#1F1B18`) and dynamic timer countdown in bold Terracotta (`#C25E2E`).
  - Action Footer: Full-width button nestled with 16px inset.

### Spec Sheets & Health Verification Modules
- **Health Verification Records:**
  - Container: Mint-tinted well (`#ECFDF5`), 1px solid border (`#A7F3D0`), 12px radius.
  - Content: Verified green checkmark icon (`#059669`) paired with certificate title (e.g., "Vaccinated – Goat Pox") and validation dates.
- **Metadata Spec Pills:**
  - Small tactile badges (`Species: Goat`, `Age: 2 Years`, `Gender: Male`) utilizing `#FAF8F5` background, `#E7E1D8` border, and dark charcoal values.

### Navigation Header & Dark Footer
- **Header:**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFBF7` with bottom hairline border `#E7E1D8`.
  - Brand Mark: Bold typography with dark chestnut logotype.
  - Navigation Links: 15px font weight 500 in `#1F1B18`, with active indicator marked by an underline in `#643822` or `#C25E2E`.
- **Dark Footer:**
  - Background: Solid deep charcoal `#120F0D`.
  - Links: Muted sand `#A8A29E`, turning `#FAF8F5` on hover.
  - Column Heads: Pure white or soft cream `#FDFBF7` with bold weight.
